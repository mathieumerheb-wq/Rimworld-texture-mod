# Human penis/balls redraw — not used (2026-11-25)

Two attempts at improving Sized Apparel Zero's human `Penis_*` sprites (Penis, Horny, Balls, Uncut, Foreskin):

- `HumanRemaster/`: SA's own picture re-rendered at 4x (smoother edges, thinner outline). The gain was barely
  visible and fine details were lost. Script: `Build-HumanRemaster.py`.
- `HumanRedraw/`: new shading on SA's footprint (volume lighting, glans highlight, veins, scrotal seam).
  Script: `Build-HumanRedraw.py` (`all` builds every slot).

The user judged SA's originals better overall and kept them. Both sets keep SA's exact footprint, so they
would still fit the placement fix if revisited. The scripts read from the installed SA folder.
Known flaws of the redraw: lumpy glans on aroused size 5 side, a faint light ring on rest size 5 front, weak
foreskin pucker.
